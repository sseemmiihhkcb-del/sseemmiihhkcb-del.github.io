Privacy Policy – War Survivor Last Stand

Last updated: October 14, 2026

This policy describes how "War Survivor Last Stand" ("the app"), published by Fizzle Games, handles information.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email or precise location. Game progress (gold, diamonds, unlocked characters and maps, best scores, settings) is stored locally on your device and is not sent to our servers.

Third-party services

The app uses third-party services that may collect and process certain data:

  • Google AdMob (advertising): may collect the advertising identifier, device information, IP address and ad interaction data to show and measure ads, including optional rewarded ads.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (notifications): uses a device/installation identifier and push token to deliver push notifications.
  • Google Play Billing / Unity IAP (in-app purchases): purchases are processed by Google Play (or the Apple App Store on iOS). We do not receive or store your payment details.
  • Unity Gaming Services: may process device information required to operate in-app purchases.

These services have their own privacy policies:

  • Google
  • Google AdMob data collection
  • Firebase
  • Unity
  • Apple

Advertising

The app shows ads. On Android you can reset or opt out of ad personalization in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads). On iOS the app asks for permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before the advertising identifier is used; if you decline, ads may still be shown but will not be personalized. You can change this choice at any time in your device settings.

Push notifications

The app may ask for permission to send notifications, for example about game updates. You can turn notifications off at any time in your device settings.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Data deletion

We do not keep personal data on our own servers. You can remove locally stored data by uninstalling the app. For any request, contact us.

Changes

We may update this policy; the latest version will be posted on this page.
