About the game

Darkness has fallen. Endless waves of skeletons, trolls, minotaurs and dark mages are closing in — and you are the last one standing. Slash through the hordes, collect XP gems, pick new powers as you level up and survive as long as you can.

  • 4 unique heroes – Warrior, Fantasy Knight, Horseman and Orc Assassin
  • Combos & skills – 3-hit combos, arrow storms, explosive damage, wind auras
  • Boss fights – a stronger boss arrives every few minutes
  • 7 maps – from the Grassland to the Crimson Keep and the Cursed Swamp
  • Permanent upgrades – Health, Damage, Armor, Magnet and Gold Gain
  • Daily missions, achievements and free gold & diamond chests
