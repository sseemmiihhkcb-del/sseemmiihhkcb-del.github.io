Privacy Policy – Iron Survivor: Mech War

Last updated: September 25, 2026

This policy describes how "Iron Survivor: Mech War" ("the app"), published by Fizzle Games, handles information.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email or precise location. Game progress (gold, diamonds, unlocks, settings) is stored locally on your device.

Third-party services

The app uses third-party services that may collect and process certain data:

  • Google AdMob (advertising): may collect the Android Advertising ID, device information and IP address to show and measure ads.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (notifications): uses a device/installation identifier to deliver push notifications.
  • Google Play Billing / Unity IAP (in-app purchases): purchases are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details.
  • Unity Gaming Services: may process device information required to operate the app's services.

These services have their own privacy policies:

  • Google
  • Firebase
  • Unity

Advertising

The app shows ads. You can reset or opt out of ad personalization in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Data deletion

We do not keep personal data on our own servers. You can remove locally stored data by uninstalling the app. For any request, contact us.

Changes

We may update this policy; the latest version will be posted on this page.
